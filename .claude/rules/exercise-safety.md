# Exercise Safety Rules

## Non-Negotiable Constraints
- Never generate or imply medical advice — always include: "Consult a qualified professional before starting any fitness program."
- No diagnostic language about injuries — describe symptoms generically, redirect to professionals.
- All rep ranges and load recommendations must cite their source methodology (e.g., NSCA, ACSM).

## Rep Range Standards
| Goal | Sets | Reps | Rest |
|------|------|------|------|
| Strength | 3–6 | 1–6 | 3–5 min |
| Hypertrophy | 3–5 | 6–12 | 60–90 s |
| Endurance | 2–4 | 12–20+ | 30–60 s |
| Power | 3–5 | 1–5 | 3–5 min |

## Progressive Overload Gates
- Max weekly load increase: 10% (novice), 5% (intermediate), 2.5% (advanced)
- Deload trigger: 4–6 weeks of accumulation, or any form breakdown signal
- Never auto-increase load on an exercise flagged with a form warning

## 1RM Estimation
- Use Epley formula by default: `weight × (1 + reps/30)`
- Display as estimate with ±10% confidence range
- Never use 1RM estimates for warm-up load prescriptions directly — apply % tables

## Form Cue Quality
- Every exercise must have at minimum: setup, execution, and common error cues
- Cues must be action-oriented ("brace your core") not outcome-described ("be stable")
- Flag any exercise without validated form cues as `needs-review`

## Contraindications
- Pre-screen for: recent surgery, pregnancy, cardiovascular conditions, joint replacements
- If any contraindication flag is present, restrict exercise recommendations to low-impact, recommend professional consultation
